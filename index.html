1/4 • Student Marketplace

Tutor Shop was built because private STEM tutoring was a hassle: scheduling was messy, nobody was accountable, marketing was non-existent, and payments were manual. It was built in three stages to avoid spending money on something people didn't want:

Phase 0: Testing Demand Carrd.co

Put up free landing pages to see if people would pay before building anything. Each tutor had a profile with a booking button, and requests were handled by hand.

Archive: thetutorshop.carrd.co ↗ • Tutor Directory ↗
Phase 1: First Working Version & Branding Softr.ai • Google Sheets → Airtable

Created the brand (teal and white, logo) and built a marketplace where students could browse and filter tutors on their own. Started on Google Sheets, then moved to Airtable to track tutors, hourly rates, and subjects.

Archive: thetutorshop.softr.app ↗
Phase 2: Custom-Built Platform (Current) React • Supabase • Claude Opus • Lovable

Rebuilt the platform as a React web app, with Supabase handling the database, logins, and session records. Built with AI tools (Claude Opus through Lovable), with Google Stitch and Gemini used to brainstorm the design and layouts.

  • Organization Accounts: Schools and tutoring agencies can set up their own mini-site inside Tutor Shop. Each gets an admin dashboard, roster controls, custom branding, and its own booking page with payments built in.
  • Daily Word Games: Added daily word puzzles to give students a reason to come back every day and make learning more fun.
  • Scheduling & Billing: Built the scheduling and session tracking for 50+ students and 20+ verified tutors. It also handles client billing, tutor payouts, and payment disputes, so the numbers stay accurate.

Multi-Timeframe Market Indicator

Pine Script • Technical Trading

May 2025 — Jun 2025
TradingView Indicator Visualization assets/images/project-indicator.png

A trading indicator that combines momentum and trend signals from two timeframes to cut out noise and spot better setups.

  • Combined 3-minute MACD signals with a 15-minute RSI filter to sort trades into four types of reversals and continuations. This made signals about 80% more reliable.
