Tutor Shop

3-Stage Evolution

Production Marketplace • Founder & Platform Architect

Live: tutorshop.ca

Founded and engineered Tutor Shop to solve scheduling bottlenecks, lack of accountability, and manual payment friction in private STEM tutoring. To maintain engineering discipline and mitigate capital risk, the platform was developed across three deliberate architectural phases:

Phase 0: Demand Validation & Concept Carrd.co

Deployed zero-cost responsive landing pages to validate consumer willingness to pay before writing production code. Structured independent tutor profiles with direct booking CTAs and manual inquiry pipelines.

Archive: thetutorshop.carrd.co ↗ • Tutor Directory ↗
Phase 1: Database-Driven MVP & Brand Identity Softr.ai • Google Sheets → Airtable

Established the brand identity (teal green & white palette, official logo) and engineered an interactive self-serve marketplace. Connected dynamic front-end filtering through Google Sheets and transitioned into an Airtable relational database to manage tutor rosters, hourly rates, and subject tags.

Archive: thetutorshop.softr.app ↗
Phase 2: Full-Stack Production Architecture & Enterprise Systems (Current) React • Supabase • Claude Opus • Lovable

Re-engineered the platform into a custom full-stack React web application backed by Supabase for relational data storage, authentication, and session records. Leveraged rapid AI-assisted development (Claude Opus via Lovable) and utilized Google Stitch AI and Gemini for user experience brainstorming, component layout schemas, and iterative concept design.

  • Multi-Tenant Organization Management CMS: Architected a dedicated enterprise subsystem that allows educational institutions, schools, and tutoring agencies to spin up their own isolated mini-platform within Tutor Shop. Features a private administrative dashboard, roster controls, custom branding, and a dedicated booking page with embedded automated payment routing.
  • Daily Word Games & Engagement Engine: Engineered custom daily word puzzle challenges directly into the web application to drive continuous organic traffic, gamify educational problem-solving, and cultivate consistent daily active user (DAU) retention.
  • Core Operations & Billing: Architected automated scheduling logic and session-tracking systems serving 50+ students with 20+ verified tutors, managing client billing, contractor payouts, and dispute resolution workflows to guarantee fiscal accuracy.
